## 5. Source-use and copyright policy

- The archive is the user's private copy. The app stores concise study notes, vocabulary
  records, and **original** exercises grounded in the book — not large verbatim passages.
- Short snippets (a word, a phrase, an example sentence pattern) are kept with page refs.
- OCR/reference files live in `vlm_ocr/` and are never bundled into the app.

## 6. Uncertainties / items marked for manual verification

- OCR of small kana inline with romaji (e.g. `Wataし`) is generally handled, but individual
  items may still be misread. Any curriculum item that could not be confirmed from the page
  text is marked `"verified": false` in its JSON and **excluded from scored questions**
  until checked.
- Root `vlm_ocr/ocr_errors.csv` 404 rows are superseded pipeline errors (see §1), not
  content gaps.
- No audio assets exist → no listening exercises in v1 (architecture ready).

## 9. Game progression and boss rules

Each non-reference curriculum section is divided into category-based level
nodes using its existing verified practice items. Nodes are capped at five
questions where possible; categories are derived from exercise `skill` values
(e.g. writing systems, pronunciation, sound patterns, vocabulary, grammar, and
sentence building). A mixed Mastery Review is placed immediately before the
final Boss Battle. Level IDs are category-based and persistent; the displayed
`unit-level` number follows curriculum order, including the four pre-lessons.

Ordinary levels have no timer or lives. Each question accepts one answer for
that run; missed questions are not replayed individually. A player must achieve
at least 80% accuracy to clear the level. A failed level can be restarted in
full, and later nodes remain locked until the previous node is cleared.

The unit boss uses a custom-drawn Frost Shrine Guardian sprite, 100 boss
HP, three player hearts, and a 12-second timer per question. Each correct
answer deals 20 damage; a wrong answer or timeout costs one heart. Defeating
the unit boss records section completion, awards the normal section completion
reward once, and unlocks the next curriculum section. The Home screen's
Cumulative Review remains a separate optional exam.

Stage completion is saved in the existing SharedPreferences progress document.
Older saves remain compatible because absent stage-completion data defaults to
an empty set. Flutter runtime behavior still requires verification by running
`flutter analyze`, `flutter test`, and `flutter build web` in a Flutter-enabled
environment.
