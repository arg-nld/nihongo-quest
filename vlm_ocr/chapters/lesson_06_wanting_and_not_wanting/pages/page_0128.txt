Making adjectives negative
In the prior lesson you learned how to make nouns negative by adding ja ない to them. For
example, いぬ ja ないです, means “it is not a dog.” To make い adjectives negative, ~ja な
い can not be used.

All true adjectives end with an い. To make い adjectives negative, drop the last い then add
くない.

(い adjective) minus い, add くない
It's not (adjective)

Examples
1. あtaらしい = new
あtaらしくない = not new

3. さmuい = cold
さmuくない = not cold

5. つmeたい = cold to the touch
つmeたくない = not cold to the touch

2. おいしい = delicious
おいしくない = not delicious

4. あつい = hot
あつくない = not hot

6. furuい = old
furuくない = not old

The colors as negative adjectives
The same thing can be done with the adjective forms of colors.

Examples
1. あかい = red
あかくない = not red

3. chaいroい = brown
chaいろくない = not brown

2. あおい = blue
あおくない = not blue

4. きいroい = yellow
きいroくない = not yellow
