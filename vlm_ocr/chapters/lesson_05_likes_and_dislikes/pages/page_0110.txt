❑ Using jana い to make things negative
Jana い means “not” or “don’t” depending on the context of the sentence. Jana い is the
informal version of ja あ rima せ n 、de wa あ rima せ n 、or で wa na い however the
informal version is most commonly used in everyday conversation. Jana い on its own is
informal, but can be made polite by adding です. It must always follow the word it is
modifying.

[word] jana いです。
It's not a [word].

Examples
1. あか jana いです。    It's not red.
2. Wa たし jana いです。    It's not me.
3. た na かさ n jana いです。    It is not Mr. Tanaka.
4. そ re jana いです。    It's not that one.
5. すき jana いです。    I don't like it.
6. き ra い jana いです。    I don't dislike it.

❑ The "one" pronoun
When the particle no comes after the noun version of a color or after an adjective, it
transforms the word into what is called the “one” pronoun.

Examples
1. あか no    (a / the) red one
2. cha い ro no    (a / the) brown one
3. おいしい no    (a / the) good tasting one
4. nihon no    (a / the) Japanese one
5. mura さき と き い ro no    (a / the) purple and yellow one

Example Q&A
1. ど re が すきですか。    Which one do you like?
   あか no が すきです。    I like the red one.
   おいしい no が すきです。    I like the delicious one.

2. あ na た no ne こ wa ど re ですか。    Which one is your cat?
   Cha い ro no です。    It's the brown one.
   く ro と orenji no です。    It's the black and orange one.
