❑ Double consonants
Some Japanese words use double consonant sounds. Double consonants such as 'kk',
'pp', 'tt', and 'cch' must be stressed more than a single consonant to show the correct
meaning of a word.

Examples
roku    number six
rokku    rock (music)

uta    a song
utta    sold (past tense verb)

mata    again
matta    waited (past tense verb)
