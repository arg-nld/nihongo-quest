あ How this book works

Japanese From Zero! uses Japanese From Zero’s PROGRESSIVE SYSTEM of teaching
hiragana. As you learn new hiragana, we will immediately replace the roman letters (ro–maji)
with the hiragana you have just learned. For example, after you learn あ (which sounds like
“ah”) we will mix it into the example words.
