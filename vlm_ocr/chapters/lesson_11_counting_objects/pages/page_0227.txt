Hiragana: The Next Step

Congratulations on learning hiragana!
Here are some tips to help you reinforce what you have learned:

Let's put hiragana in our daily lives!
Write words in hiragana on “post it” notes and then stick them on items around your house.
You can even write しお and こしょう on your salt and pepper shakers using a permanent
marker. This will enforce your skills even when you aren't thinking about it.

[Image of chair with note reading いす]
[Image of calculator with note reading けいさんき]
[Image of cat with note reading ねこ]

Read manga!
Some manga (Japanese comics) and children's books will have small hiragana next to any
kanji used in dialogue. When hiragana is used in this way it is called “furigana”. Look for
furigana when purchasing manga to help you study. It's like Japanese on training wheels!

[Image of waitress with speech bubble containing 何(なに)を飲(の)む？]

Keep on learning!
Your next step is to learn katakana! You have come this far, so keep up the momentum.
