7 Word Usage ことばの つかいかた

☐ Yo くない vs いくない
In Lesson 6 you learned how to make any adjective negative by dropping the い and adding くない.

Examples
1. おおきい = big
おっきくない = not big
2. ちいさい = small
ちいさくない = not small

いい is an exception to the normal pattern. Both いい and yoi mean "nice, good".
And even though you will sometimes hear people say いくない to mean "not good" in a
casual conversation, it is not commonly used and generally not considered standard
Japanese. Instead, yo くない should be used to say "not good".

7 New Phrases あたらしい かいわ

1. おなかがいすいています。
This literally means "My stomach is empty."
I'm hungry.

2. おなかがいっぱいです。
This literally means "My stomach is full."
I'm full.

3. のどがかわいています。
This literally means "My throat is dry."
I'm thirsty.
