7 About This Lesson このレッスンについて

Before The Lesson
1. Be able to write and read はひふへほ, ばびぶべぼ, ぱぴぷぺぽ.
2. Understand how make a noun into a modifying word using the particle の.
3. Review vocabulary group I.

Lesson Goals
1. Learn how to say where an item is.
2. Learn when to use は (wa) or が (ga).

From The Teachers
1. The こそあど diagram in Lesson 2 for will be helpful in understanding the distance relationships for ここ, そこ, and あそこ words in the どこ word group in this lesson.
