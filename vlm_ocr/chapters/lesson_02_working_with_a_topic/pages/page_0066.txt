2 Sentence Building ぶんのつくり

In each lesson we build on a previous sentence. Watch it grow and transform each time
new concepts are introduced.

The sentence from the previous lesson was: Nan desuか. (What is it?). In this lesson we
learned how to attach a topic to the question. Let's look at the new sentence:

こre wa nan desuか.
What is this?
