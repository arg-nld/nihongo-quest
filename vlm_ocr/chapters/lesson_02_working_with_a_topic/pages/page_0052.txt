topic marker

Using wa in a variety of sentences
Remember that wa is used to mark the topic of a sentence. Read the examples to see how
wa can be applied to the words that you already know. Don't forget that the word before the
wa is the topic, and everything after wa is asking a question about or describing the topic.

Example Q&A
1.  Kore wa nan desu ka.    What is this?
    Sore wa kagami desu.    That is a mirror.

2.  Sore wa nan desu ka.    What is that?
    Sore wa pen desu.    That is a pen.
    あ re wa banana desu.    That over there is a banana.

3.  Kore wa yasa い desu ka.    Is this a vegetable?
    いいえ、sore wa furu–tsu desu.    No, that is a fruit.

4.  Banana wa nan desu ka.    What is a banana?
    Banana wa furu–tsu desu.    A banana is a fruit.

5.  Retasu wa furu–tsu desu ka.    Is lettuce a fruit?
    いいえ、retasu wa yasa い desu.    No, lettuce is a vegetable.
