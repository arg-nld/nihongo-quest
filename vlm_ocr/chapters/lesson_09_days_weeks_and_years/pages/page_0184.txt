9 Culture Clip: Japan's New Year

In Japan, the New Year is the most important holiday period. It is as important in Japan as Christmas is in the United States. From January 1st until one week after, Japan almost comes to a stop. Most stores and companies are closed while everyone celebrates the New Year.
