Japanese From Zero! – Lesson 3 – Possession 75

Now let's put your new knowledge of no into practice. These sentences may appear long at
first, but they're using the simple grammar you've learned already.

Example Sentences
1. Anata no kaのjo no namaえ wa nan desu か。
What is your girlfriend's name?

2. Tanaか san no おかあ san wa nansai desu か。
How old is Tanaka san's mother?

3. Koばyashi san no おtoう san wa dare desu か。
Who is Kobayashi san's father?

3 Q&A しつもん と こたえ E→J

1. Who is it?	Dare desu か。
It's me.	Watashi desu.
It's Hideko.	Hideこ desu.
It's Mom.	おかあ san desu.

2. Whose car is it?	Dare no ＜ruma desu か。
It's my car.	Watashi no ＜ruma desu.
It's mine.	Watashi no desu.
It's Mr. Takada's car.	Taかda san no ＜ruma desu.
I don't know.	Waか rimasen.

3. Which one is your rabbit?	Docchi が anata no うsaぎ desu か。
That one over there is my rabbit.	あcchi が watashi no うsaぎ desu.
It's that one.	Socchi desu.

4. Is this your car?	こre wa anata no ＜ruma desu か。
Yes, it is.	Haい, soう desu.
No, it's my friend's car.	いいえ、tomodachi no ＜ruma desu.
No, it's her car.	いいえ、kaのjo no ＜ruma desu.
No, it's hers.	いいえ、kaのjo no desu.

5. Which one is your bird?	Anata no tori wa dore desu か。
My bird is that one.	Boく no tori wa sore desu.
