3 Q&A しつもん と こたえ J→E

1. Anata no neこ wa dore desu か。	Which one is your cat?
Watashi no neこ wa sore desu.	My cat is that one.
Watashi no neこ wa あre desu.	My cat is that one over there.

2. おかあ san no namaえ wa nan desu か。	What is (your) mother's name?
Maria desu.	It's Maria.
Yoshiこ desu.	It's Yoshiko.

3. Sore wa kaのjo no taoru desu か。	Is that her towel?
いいえ、kaれ no taoru desu.	No, it's his towel.
いいえ、Mamiこ san no taoru desu.	No, it's Mamiko's towel.

4. Anata no tomodachi no iぬ nu no	What is your friend's dog's name?
namaえ wa nan desu か。
Iぬ nu no namaえ wa Pochi desu.	The dog's name is Pochi.
Waか rimasen.	I don't know.
