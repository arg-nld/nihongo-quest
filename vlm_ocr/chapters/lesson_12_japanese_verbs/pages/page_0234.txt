12 Grammar ぶんぽう
Polite versus informal speaking
In Japanese, it is common to use polite language with people you don't know, or who have 
higher social status than yourself. For example, when speaking to a stranger on the street 
or to your boss, you should use polite verb forms.

However, if you are talking to your family, friends, or people that are of lower or equal 
status than you, speaking informally will be more natural. Until your Japanese level 
becomes more advanced, we suggest that you stick to polite Japanese in order to form 
good speaking habits.
