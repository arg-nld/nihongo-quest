The forms

Notice how the rows on the chart above have been labeled あ, い, う, え, お form.
You may have noticed that every new verb in this lesson ends with a hiragana in the
う form. This isn't just a coincidence – because all verbs in the Japanese language in
the dictionary form end with う form hiragana.

For example, the verb いく (to go) ends with く. くる (to come), わかる (to understand),
and かえる (to return) all end in る. Both く and る are う form hiragana.
