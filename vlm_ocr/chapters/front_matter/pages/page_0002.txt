George Trombley has worked as a professional Japanese interpreter for over 20 years
specializing in business, technical, medical, and entertainment simultaneous interpreting. In
1998, George and Yukari Takenaka created Japanese From Zero’s live Japanese language
classes in Las Vegas, NV, and developed their classes into the Japanese From Zero! book
series and the YesJapan.com interactive Japanese language education website.
