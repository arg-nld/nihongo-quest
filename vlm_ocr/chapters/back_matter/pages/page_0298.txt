☐ Lesson 13: Short dialogue translation
Mr. Watanabe: Are you also working from 8:00 tomorrow, Mr. Hashimoto?
Mr. Hashimoto: No, tomorrow I'm off.
Mr. Watanabe: That's nice. Where are you going, tomorrow?
Mr. Hashimoto: I'm going to Osaka.
Mr. Watanabe: (By) airplane?
Mr. Hashimoto: Yes, (by) the nine o'clock plane. I'm going to the airport at eight.
Mr. Watanabe: Is that so? When are you coming back?
Mr. Hashimoto: I'm coming back to Tokyo on next Tuesday.
