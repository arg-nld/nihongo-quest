❑ Lesson 3: Reading comprehension questions
Question	Answer
1. Is Honda's girlfriend's name Tanaka?	いいえ、Terada deす.*
2. Who is Terada?	Hondaさn no かnojo deす.
3. Is Terada Yamada's girlfriend?	いいえ、Honda saun no かnojo deす.
	OR いいえ、chiがいmaす.
4. How old is Ms. Terada?	Nijuう kyuうさい deす.

* (Note: いいえ、Honda saun no かnojo no nameえ wa in front of the answer above would be
unaturally long. Try to keep the answers simple.)

❑ Lesson 3: Short dialogue translation
Yoshida san:	Good afternoon, Ms. Ueki.
Ueki san:	Good afternoon, Mr. Yoshida. It's been a long time.
	Is your father doing well?
Yoshida san:	Yes he is fine. What is that?
Ueki san:	This is a camera. It's my hobby.
Yoshida san:	That's great!
Ueki san:	What is your (Yoshida san's) hobby?
Yoshida san:	My hobby is golf.
