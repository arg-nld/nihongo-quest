❑ Lesson 2: Hiragana matching
き	i

❑ Lesson 2: Question and answer 1
1. What is this?	(こre wa)くruma desu.
2. What is this?	(こre wa) konpyu-ta- desu.
3. Is this a mirror?	いいえ、(こre wa) mado desu.
4. Are these vegetables?	いいえ、(こre wa) furu-tsu desu.

❑ Lesson 2: Question and answer 2
1. Which is a book?	(1st picture) Kore desu.
2. Which is a vegetable?	(middle picture: onion) Kore desu.
3. Which is a fruit?	(2nd picture: cherries) Kocchi desu.

❑ Lesson 2: Question and answer 3
1. Q: What is that over there?	A: That's a dog.
(あre wa) nan desu か。	あre waいnu desu.

2. Q: What is this?	A: That's a toothbrush
こre wa nan desu か。	(Sore wa) haburashi desu.

3. Q: Is that a pillow?	A: No, this is a towel.
Sore wa makura desu か。	いいえ、こre wa taoru desu.

4. Q: What is that?	A: This is a book.
Sore wa nan desu か。	(こre wa) hon desu.

❑ Lesson 2: Japanese translation
1. Satoko san:	Is that a book?
Shouta san:	No, it is a magazine.
Satoko san:	Please say it one more time. What is it?
Shouta san:	This is a magazine.
