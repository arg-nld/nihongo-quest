☐ Lesson 13: Japanese translation
1. A: From when do you work? / When does your work start?
B: It starts from February 8th.
A: From what time until what time is the job?
B: It is from 8:00 A.M. to 4:00 P.M.

2. A: What time is it now?
B: 4:20.
A: I am going home at 4:30.
B: Where is your house?
A: It's over there.

3. A: I am returning to Japan from tomorrow. (perfectly correct sentence in Japanese.)
B: Until when?
A: Until August 15th.
B: Is that so? That's nice.
