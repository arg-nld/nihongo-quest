❑ Lesson 3: Question and answer 1
Question	Answer
1. Who is this?	Yoしdaさn deす.
2. Is this Jenny?	いいえ、Monikaさn deす.
3. Whose birds are these?	Taかこsan no tori deす.
4. Whose mother is this?	Makoto (くn) no おかあさn deす.
