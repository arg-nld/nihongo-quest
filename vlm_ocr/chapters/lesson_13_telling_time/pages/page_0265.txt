Japanese From Zero! - Lesson 13 - Lesson Activities 265

□ English translation
Translate the following conversations into Japanese.

1.
Mr. A: When did you come here?
Ms. B: I came at nine.
Mr. A: Where is your friend?
Ms. B: My friend didn't come.
Mr. A: Where is your friend now?
Ms. B: He is at home now.

2.
Mrs. A: What time is your airplane?
Mr. B: It's 10:30 a.m.
Mrs. A: What time are you going to the airport?
Mr. B: I'll go at 9:00.
Mrs. A: Got it.
