4 Culture Clip: Green is Blue?

Blue
apples?

Japanese people sometimes refer to what we call “green” as “blue.”
For example, in Japan, when vegetables are not ripe they are referred
to as blue, not green. A green apple isn't green – it’s blue. Green
traffic lights are called “blue” even though the colors used for traffic
lights in Japan are exactly the same as in America. So keep this in
mind when someone yells to you, “Hey, let's go! The light is blue!”

There is a prefecture in the northern part of Japan’s main island
Honshuu called Aomori, which literally means “blue forest.” Well then, what color is the sky in
Japan, you ask? Blue, of course!
