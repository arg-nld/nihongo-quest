4 About This Lesson このレッスンについて

Before The Lesson
1. Be able to write and read さしすせそ, ざじずぜぞ.
2. Understand how to use the question word dare and the particles no and が.
3. Review vocabulary group E.

Lesson Goals
1. Learn how to use the noun and adjective forms of colors.
2. Learn how to use the Japanese version of "and".

From The Teachers
1. Memorize the colors.
2. Make sure that you understand the different types of adjectives introduced in this lesson.
