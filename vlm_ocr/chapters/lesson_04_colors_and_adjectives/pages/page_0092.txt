□ Using colors to describe
In this lesson, the colors are introduced in their noun form. To make the color an adjective
you must link the words with no. To say "green car" in Japanese, you would say miどり no
く ruma. This type of adjective is called a no adjective. With the noun forms of colors you
CANNOT say, for example, miどり く ruma to mean "green car."
