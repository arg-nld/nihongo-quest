4 Grammar ぶんぽう

□ The particle to
To is the equivalent of English "and." It must come between every word in a list. To cannot
be used to link sentences together.

Examples
1. あお to mura さき to pinku
2. wataし to nata to tana かさ n
3. い nu to ne こ to う ma to buta

blue, purple, and pink.
me, you and Tanaka san
a dog, a cat, a horse, and a pig

Example Sentences
1. Wataし no く ruma wa く ro to ぎ n い ro で す。
My car is black and sliver.

2. Tomodachi no ne こ wa cha い ro to し ro で す。

□ Making nouns into adjectives using no
Up until now, no has been used to mark possession. No is also used to create a "noun
adjective" or what we call a "no adjective". When no is added after a noun, that noun
becomes an adjective. The noun that comes before the no describes the word that comes
after the no.

[noun 1] + no + [noun 2]

Examples
1. Japanese food
nihon (Japan) + tabemono (food) = nihon no tabemono

2. American cars
Amerika (America) + く ruma = Amerika no く ruma

3. Japanese book
nihonご (Japanese language) + hon (book) = nihonご no hon
