D Speaking Naturally しぜんに はなすこと

❑ The silent "U" sound
Many phrases taught in this lesson end with "masu".

Examples
1. Ohayou gozaimasu.
2. itte kimasu.
3. Arigatou gozaimasu.

In everyday spoken Japanese, the final "u" in words containing "masu" is silent and the
word is pronounced as "mas". Practice your words and phrases with this pronunciation and
you will sound more natural.
