や Special Usage とくべつな つかいかた

❏ The particle を (wo)
The hiragana を is only used as a particle (object marker). It is never used for any other
purpose. Even though "wo" is normally pronounced "o", お can never replace を as a
particle.

 Example Sentences
 1. てがみを (wo) かきます。                I will write a letter.
 2. えんぴつを (wo) ください。              Give me a pencil please.
