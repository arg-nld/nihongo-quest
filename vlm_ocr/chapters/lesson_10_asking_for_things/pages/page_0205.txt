Compound Hiragana

The final hiragana are easy! There are only 33 official hiragana left to learn - but don't let that number scare you. They are all made up of the hiragana that you already know. Just by looking at them you should already have an idea of the sound that they represent.

Examples
き (ki) + や (ya) = きゃ (kya)
し (shi) + ゆ (yu) = しゅ (shu)
ち (chi) + よ (yo) = ちょ (cho)

The correct way to write compound hiragana
When writing compound hiragana, make sure that the second character is visibly smaller than the first character.
